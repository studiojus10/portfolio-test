About

关于我
PORTRAIT 个人肖像

J ustin Hughes is a digital media and communications professional whose creative perspective has been shaped by international education, culinary arts, language immersion, music, travel, and high-touch hospitality. His work sits at the intersection of culture and communication: understanding how people connect, what makes experiences memorable, and how story, design, sound, food, and language influence trust.

Unlike a conventional digital marketer, Justin brings lived cultural fluency to his creative work. He has studied in France and China, trained in elite culinary environments, achieved advanced Mandarin proficiency, developed French-language capability, worked in a three-Michelin-star restaurant, and built client-facing service experiences rooted in empathy and human connection.
