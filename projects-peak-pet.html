Peak Pet and Home Care

顶峰宠物护理

Peak Pet and Home Care is a local Colorado Springs company offering in-home pet sitting, home watch, and daily dog walks. This project demonstrates a complete set of advertising and brand materials designed for a small local business, to establish a cohesive visual identity and then applying it across a mock website and social media campaigns. The goal was to build a brand language that communicates trust, warmth, and professionalism to Colorado Springs families looking for dependable care for their pets and homes.

Brand Guidelines

The brand guidelines define the visual foundation that all other materials are built on — logo usage, color palette, typography, and tone of voice. Every subsequent piece, from the website to the social ads, is derived from this document.
