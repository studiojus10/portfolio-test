Video

电影感叙事

A concise showcase of motion, rhythm, and cinematic tone.

Crafting moving images with editorial precision.

Every edit and frame is designed to read like a still image in motion. The red accent is used to punctuate energy, while the black-and-white palette creates contrast between stillness and movement.

A Proposal in Dublin, Ireland

都柏林求婚

A personal artistic film documenting an intimate moment in Dublin, scored with original music composed and performed by the filmmaker.

Winter Scene

冬景

A collage of winter footage captured through an anamorphic lens — a nostalgic and cinematic study of Colorado snow, light, and stillness.

A Collage of Found Footage

发现素材拼贴

Fragments of incidental footage, old TV commercials and PSAs assembled with narrative logic, tied together with narration — an experiment in how meaning emerges from juxtaposition and perspective.

Portrait of a Musician

音乐家肖像

An observational study of a musician at work — camera lingering on hands, breath, and the endless dedication required for perfection, rather than the performance itself. A story is told through intimate efforts of preperation and narrated letter from the musician to her best friend.

A Study of Light in One Shot

一镜光影研究

A single unbroken take with no edit — the subject is light itself, shifting, changing, and captivating an unknown spectator.

Resurrection

复活

An experimental short built around transformation and return — form dissolving and reassembling through image and sound rather than story. A visual set to a poem.

Murder, He Wrote

他所写的谋杀

A narrative short in the noir tradition — shadow, unreliable language, and a plot that unfolds more like a confession than a story.

Decadance

颓废之舞

A narrative short where movement becomes the language — choreography, indulgence, and consequence told through the body rather than dialogue.
